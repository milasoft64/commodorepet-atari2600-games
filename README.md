Castlesmash

<img width="710" height="710" alt="picture" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/590ad99c-d571-495e-bc34-c6f9ed0545fb" />

A one to four player game in which you defend your castles from fireballs and try to deflect them onto your opponents. For Commodore PET and Commodore 64.
